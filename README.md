## <samp>🧭 About</samp>

I'm an **AI/ML engineer** with a **Bachelor of Software Engineering**, and I sit
exactly where *research* meets *production*. I train and fine-tune deep learning
models, then do the unglamorous work that actually matters — evaluation harnesses,
data pipelines, deployment, monitoring — until the thing ships.

I care about models that survive contact with real users, not just notebooks
that hit a leaderboard number. Currently deep in **LLM fine-tuning, RAG systems,
and MLOps**, with a soft spot for building the mobile front-ends those models
deserve.
